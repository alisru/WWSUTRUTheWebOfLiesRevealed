Privacy Notice & Research Architecture

This research platform is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Mandatory Personal Info: You are never required to provide your legal name, email, phone number, or password.
  • Anonymous Authentication: Submissions use anonymous cryptographic session tokens so you can revisit and edit your response without creating an account.
  • Local Storage: In-progress answers are saved in your browser's local storage so you don't lose progress if you refresh.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not use advertising networks, tracking beacons, or cross-site fingerprinting.
  • Data Ownership: You can download your full response as a JSON or PDF file at completion.
