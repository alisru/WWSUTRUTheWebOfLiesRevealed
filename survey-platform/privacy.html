Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Privacy Notice & Research Architecture

We believe civic research and public consensus mapping must protect respondent sovereignty. Our survey platform is architected from the ground up to minimize data collection, prevent surveillance profiling, and empower you with full ownership of your contributions.

1. No Mandatory Personal Information

You are never required to provide your real name, email address, phone number, home address, or social security details, we don't want nor care for it. Any alias or geographical region you choose to enter on review screens is strictly optional and used solely for aggregate geographic grouping and for the purpose of personal identification

2. Anonymous Session Authentication

To enable you to edit or review your responses without creating a user account or submitting credentials, the platform utilizes anonymous cryptographic sessions via Supabase. A randomly generated identifier is stored in your browser's private session. We do not link this identifier to your real-world identity.

3. Local Storage for In-Progress Drafts

As you navigate through survey steps, your current answers are cached directly on your device using localStorage. This ensures that accidental browser refreshes or loss of internet connectivity do not wipe your work. This data stays on your device until optional submission.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Cross-Site Tracking

We do not deploy marketing cookies, tracking pixels, ad networks, or third-party data broker analytics. Your engagement with this platform is not sold, rented, or monetized.

5. Purpose of Collected Data

Survey data (choices, numeric scales, ranking orders, Hegemony map coordinate placements, and vector trajectories) are collected to advance research on social, economic, and institutional alignment under The Web Of Lies Revealed project. Research findings may be published as anonymous open analytical reports and aggregate visualizations.

6. Data Ownership & Export

Upon completing any survey, you are provided with immediate tools to export your full submission directly to your computer as a structured JSON file (.json) or a formatted PDF printout. Your answers belong to you.

7. Inquiries & Data Rights

Because responses are submitted anonymously without personal identifiers, we cannot look up your individual responses unless you provide the unique response ID displayed on your exported confirmation file.
